01 — About

Bridging domain expertise and frontier AI.

I'm a Business Analyst and founder with 3+ years of experience translating complex domain knowledge into actionable insights for AI research initiatives — bridging quantitative disciplines like mathematics and physics with the development of agentic systems and human-AI interaction.

My work spans hospitality, banking & payments, investment banking, and global capital markets — designing analytical frameworks that evaluate AI approaches to human information-seeking, and building prototype automations with Excel, VB, Macros, and Power Automate to optimize commodity operations.

Outside of client work, I publish books, file design patents, review papers for international conferences and IEEE journals, and serve on editorial boards — a curiosity-driven approach to building beneficial AI systems.
